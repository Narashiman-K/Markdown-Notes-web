Privacy Policy

Suprasūtā Markdown Notes — web application and Android app.
Last updated 20 August 2026.

This application does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Your documents, your annotations and your settings stay on your own device. Nothing is uploaded unless you deliberately switch on one of the optional features described below, using an API key you supply yourself.

What stays on your device

The following never leave your device and are never transmitted to us, because there is no server of ours to transmit them to:

  • The documents you open, create, edit or convert
  • Annotations — highlights, underlines, strikethrough and comments
  • Your in-app document library, held in the browser's IndexedDB storage
  • Settings such as theme, zoom level and your chosen AI provider
  • Any API keys you enter

Document conversion — PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, ODT, ODS, EPUB, CSV and plain text — runs entirely inside the application. Files are never uploaded for conversion. The same is true of offline OCR: the recognition engine and its English language model are served from this application itself, so reading text out of an image works with no network connection and the image never leaves your device.

How API keys are stored

Keys are encrypted with AES-GCM before storage, using a key that the browser is instructed never to reveal to JavaScript, held in IndexedDB. This means a key cannot simply be read out of storage or scraped by most browser extensions.

We would rather be accurate than reassuring, so: this is not equivalent to operating-system-level protection. Script running on this exact origin can still ask the browser to decrypt on its behalf. It is a real improvement over storing keys as plain text, and it is not a guarantee against a compromised device.

Optional features that use the network

Each of these is off until you enable it and provide your own API key. When you use one, your request goes to that provider under your own account, governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy — not ours. We receive no copy of it.

The two relay endpoints

OpenAI and AssemblyAI refuse requests made directly from a web page, so those two features pass through small relay endpoints at /api/openai and /api/assemblyai on this site.

They are deliberately minimal and their source is public. They do not store your API key, do not log it, do not log your content, and keep no state: the request arrives, it is forwarded to the provider, the response is returned, and nothing is retained. Anthropic and Google are contacted directly from your browser and do not involve these endpoints at all. You can read the code at github.com/Narashiman-K/Markdown-Notes-web.

Web server logs

The web version is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel records standard technical request information — IP address, timestamp, the file requested and the browser's user-agent string — for delivery, security and abuse prevention. This is ordinary web-server logging, it is not linked to any identity by us, and we do not use it for analytics or profiling.

The Android app is served no pages from anywhere: it is installed on your phone and its content is bundled inside the app, so it generates no such logs.

Cookies

This application sets no cookies and uses no third-party scripts, embedded fonts or content delivery networks. Browser storage — IndexedDB, local storage and the service-worker cache — is used only to keep the application working offline and to remember your own documents and settings on your own device.

Children

This application is a general-purpose document tool. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights under the GDPR

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection in respect of personal data held about you.

In this case those rights are unusually simple to exercise, because we hold nothing about you. There is no account to close and no record to request. Everything the application holds is on your own device and under your own control:

  • Export: save any document to a file at any time.
  • Erase: clear the site data for this application in your browser settings, or uninstall the Android app. That removes your library, your settings and your stored API keys permanently.

If you have used one of the optional cloud features, the provider you chose may hold a record of that request under your own account with them. Requests about that data need to go to that provider directly, since it is held under your account and not ours.

You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU it is the authority for your country of residence.

Changes to this policy
